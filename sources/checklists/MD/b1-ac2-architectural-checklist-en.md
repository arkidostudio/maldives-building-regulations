## 2. General considerations

- [ ] The construction drawing approval and permit application form is completed correctly and in full.
- [ ] The information given on the form matches the plot (land) registry.
- [ ] It has been checked whether the plot has been subdivided.
- [ ] A foundation protection method has been submitted.
- [ ] A ventilation table has been submitted.
- [ ] A soil report has been submitted (where the building is 5 floors or taller than 18 m).
- [ ] Structural calculations have been submitted.
- [ ] The submitted drawings are drawn to the correct scales.
- [ ] All drawings are properly dimensioned.
- [ ] The required dimensions for air vents (ventilation shafts) are met.
- [ ] A lift is included where the building is more than 5 storeys or taller than 16.5 m.
- [ ] The ventilation shaft is open up to the top and is not enclosed by any floor along that height.
- [ ] A title block is included on the drawings, showing: plot/land name and island, owner's name, licensed architect, licensed engineer, date, and sheet/drawing number.
- [ ] A grid is properly shown on all plans except the key plan and location plan.
- [ ] The structural checker's and architectural checker's certificate is submitted, stamped with an unexpired stamp and signed.
- [ ] All drawings are stamped (unexpired stamp) and signed by both the structural checker and the architectural checker.

---

## 3. Further considerations

- [ ] For developments on land allocated for non-residential use, or for a residential building taller than 30.48 m, the building is designed in accordance with the set Development Controls.
- [ ] Where the Development Controls state that the building is required to prepare a concept design, it is designed in accordance with the approved concept.
- [ ] Where a Letter of No Objection from the Civil Aviation Authority is required, the building is designed as specified in that document.

---

## 4. Open space (setback)

- [ ] In islands and in islands classified as cities, where the building footprint is 1200 sq ft or more, at least 10% is left as open space (unless another clause in the regulation specifies otherwise).
- [ ] In islands classified as cities, where the footprint is between 600 and 1200 sq ft and the height is between 18.5 m and 30.48 m, 5% of the footprint is left as open space.
- [ ] In islands not classified as cities, where the plot is 1000 sq ft or more and the height is between 18.5 m and 30.48 m, 5% of the footprint is left as open space from the road-facing front of the plot.
- [ ] The 5% open space provided is at least 0.75 m in depth.
- [ ] In islands not classified as cities, where the plot is 1000 sq ft or more and the height is between 18.5 m and 24.5 m, a 0.75 m setback is left on at least two sides from the plot boundary; any balcony within it does not project more than 50% of the building length.
- [ ] Where the plot is 2000 sq ft or more and the height is between 24.5 m and 30.48 m, a 0.90 m setback is left on at least two sides from the plot boundary.
- [ ] From the start of the required open space up to the top, nothing other than balconies/eaves permitted by regulation uses that space.
- [ ] For developments on non-residential land, the open space provided is in accordance with the Development Controls / approved concept drawing.
- [ ] For developments on non-residential land, the open space is used in accordance with the Development Controls / approved concept drawing.

---

## 5. Parking

- [ ] Where the building footprint is 1200 sq ft or more and the height is above 5 storeys, parking equal to 10% of the footprint is provided.
- [ ] Where the 10% parking area is divided into multiple areas, no single portion is less than 50% of the total 10% parking area.
- [ ] The access road or ramp to the parking area is not included within the area designated as required open space.
- [ ] The designated parking area is marked clearly, with the area labelled on the drawing.
- [ ] The designated parking is laid out so that vehicles can enter and exit easily.
- [ ] Where parking is provided within the 5% open space stipulated in the regulation, no more than 50% of that open space is used for parking.
- [ ] For developments on non-residential land, the parking area is provided in accordance with the Development Controls / approved concept drawing.

---

## 6. Waste collection area

- [ ] For developments on residential land, where the building footprint is 1000 sq ft or more, at least 1.5% of the footprint is provided as a waste-collection space.
- [ ] The waste-collection space is not smaller than 2 × 2 ft.
- [ ] For non-residential land, the waste-collection space or chute is provided in accordance with the Development Controls / approved concept drawing.

---

## 7. Site plan

- [ ] The site boundary and its dimensions match the plot registry chart.
- [ ] The site boundary and building outline are marked with dimensions.
- [ ] The name and width of each street/road is clearly stated; where no name exists, "Road" is entered.
- [ ] The north point, plot area, built-up area and open area are labelled.
- [ ] The height (in storeys) of surrounding buildings is labelled.
- [ ] Ventilation shafts/airways are marked with dimensions. Where a shaft does not start at the lowest point, the floor at which it starts is shown and labelled.
- [ ] Where there is an adjacent street/alleyway, its location is clearly shown.
- [ ] Where the plot faces two roads (corner plot), the corner is curved with a radius of 12 ft (3.657 m).

---

## 8. Floor plans

*(All floors, from ground floor up to and including the terrace floor. Mark each floor's column.)*

- [ ] No part of the building projects onto the road below 9 ft.
- [ ] There is no door or window in the boundary wall that opens outward onto the road below 9 ft.
- [ ] No door above 9 ft projects/opens more than 0.457 m outward onto the road.
- [ ] No door, or opening of any kind, is placed nearer than 750 mm to the boundary of another dwelling.
- [ ] Where a balcony is on a boundary shared with another dwelling/plot, its parapet/barrier is minimum 2 m.
- [ ] All openings (doors and windows) are labelled.
- [ ] Any wall/parapet/barrier on a boundary shared with another dwelling/plot is minimum 2 m.
- [ ] Any space left adjacent to a boundary is not less than 750 mm, as required by the regulations.
- [ ] Any air shaft provided to give ventilation and natural light to parts of the building is unobstructed and not smaller than 600 × 600 mm.
- [ ] No part of the building projects into the required open space.
- [ ] The required open space is used only for window/door openings or parking.
- [ ] Where a balcony sits within the regularly allocated setback (plot 1000 sq ft or more, height between 18.5 m and 24.5 m), it does not project into the setback more than 50% of the building length.

---

## 9. Terrace plan

- [ ] For a development that reaches the maximum height and uses the terrace area for non-residential purposes, the area used does not exceed 2/3 of the total terrace area. Where this area is sheltered/covered, its height does not exceed the height of the machine room or 4 m (whichever is applicable).

---

## 10. Roof plan

- [ ] The roof gutter extension to the road is correct (within the allowed limits).
- [ ] Ventilation shafts are opened in the roof.
- [ ] For a development that reaches the maximum height, the roof does not extend more than 1.5 m beyond the roof beam.
- [ ] For a development that reaches the maximum height, the slab overhangs of the lift shaft and stairwell do not exceed 1 m.
- [ ] Where the roof projects into the open space stipulated in the regulation, the projection is not more than 0.2 m.

---

## 11. Front and side elevations

- [ ] Elevations align with the floor plans of every floor.
- [ ] The height of the building is labelled.
- [ ] Doors and windows shown match the door and window schedule.
- [ ] Balcony railings/parapets are shown correctly.
- [ ] Where 2 m walls are visible, they are shown correctly and do not exceed the permitted height.
- [ ] On side elevations, no doors or windows are shown on a boundary wall that is on a shared boundary line (with an adjacent plot).
- [ ] The ventilation shaft is open from the starting point up to the top and is not enclosed by any floor.

---

## 12. Section

- [ ] The building height is indicated and complies with the regulation.
- [ ] The height of the building is labelled.
- [ ] Drawings align with the floor plans of every floor.
- [ ] Doors and windows shown match the door and window schedule.
- [ ] Balcony railings/parapets are shown correctly.
- [ ] Where 2 m walls are visible, they are shown correctly and do not exceed the permitted height.
- [ ] No part of the building projects onto the road below 9 ft.
- [ ] No part of the building projects onto the road above 9 ft, with the exception of the roof eave.
- [ ] Where the road width is less than 6 ft, the roof eave does not project more than 0.2 m into the road.
- [ ] Where the road width is 6 ft or more, the roof eave does not project more than 0.457 m into the road.
- [ ] The floor-to-slab-soffit (clear height) in habitable spaces is 2.7 m.
- [ ] The ventilation shaft is open from the starting point up to the top and is not enclosed by any floor.
- [ ] No part of the building projects into the open space stipulated in the regulation, other than the allowable balcony projection and roof eaves.
- [ ] Where the roof projects into the open space stipulated in the regulation, the projection is not more than 0.2 m.
- [ ] Where basement parking is proposed, up to 1.5 m of additional building height is allowed, provided the ground floor is raised by no more than 1.5 m.

---

## 13. Detail drawings

- [ ] Staircase railing / wall details are submitted.
- [ ] The stair railing or wall reaches the required height (850 mm) at the stairs.
- [ ] Stair railings/walls are designed to be safe for children and cannot be climbed like a ladder.
- [ ] Balcony railing / parapet wall details are submitted.
- [ ] The balcony railing / parapet wall height is 1.2 m.
- [ ] Balcony railings do not have gaps that would admit a 125 mm (diameter) sphere.
- [ ] Balcony railings / parapet walls are designed to be safe for children and cannot be climbed like a ladder.
- [ ] Where a 2 m high wall is provided between adjacent plots, its details are submitted.
- [ ] Where the height of the stairwell and lift well exceeds 4 m above the permissible building height, lift specifications are submitted.

---

## 14. Door & window schedule

- [ ] The openable portion of windows on floors other than the ground floor is 1 m above the floor level.
- [ ] The opening direction of doors and windows is shown.
- [ ] All doors and windows labelled on the floor plans are shown in the schedule.
- [ ] All dimensions and opening directions are shown.

---

## 15. Ventilation

- [ ] The information in the ventilation schedule matches the floor plans and the door/window schedule.
- [ ] The areas given in the ventilation schedule are correct.
- [ ] Where residential areas are allocated below 7.5 m from ground level, all habitable spaces above 7.5 m fulfil the ventilation requirement.
- [ ] Where non-residential areas are allocated below 7.5 m and are mechanically ventilated, this is mentioned as such.

---

### Regulatory basis

This checklist is based principally on the **Administrative Regulations on Construction**, the **Rules on Construction in Islands/Uninhabited Islands without Planning Regulations**, the **Construction Professionals Regulations**, and their amendments as gazetted on 5 January 2021.
